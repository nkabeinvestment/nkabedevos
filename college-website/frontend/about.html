About Oakridge University

More than a century of shaping leaders, innovators, and global citizens.

Our Story

Founded in 1892,
Building the Future

Oakridge University was established with a bold vision: to create a place where brilliant minds could come together to push the boundaries of knowledge and make a meaningful difference in the world.

What began as a small liberal arts college on 50 acres of California coastline has grown into a world-renowned research university spanning 350 acres with 8 schools, 200+ research centers, and partnerships on every continent.

Today, our 35,000+ alumni include Nobel laureates, Fortune 500 CEOs, Pulitzer Prize winners, and leaders in every field imaginable.
