About Apex Ventures

We are a team of operators, founders, and investors building the premier platform for early-stage technology investing.

Our Story

From Founders,
For Founders

Apex Ventures was founded in 2015 by a group of serial entrepreneurs who had collectively built and exited over $2B in technology companies. Frustrated by the disconnect between traditional VC and the real needs of founders, they set out to build a firm that operates more like a startup accelerator than a fund.

Today, we manage over $2.5 billion across four funds and have invested in 150+ companies. Our portfolio companies have a combined valuation exceeding $18 billion, with 42 successful exits to date.

Our Philosophy

How We Think

⚖

Founder-First

We believe the best returns come from fully supporting founders. We take fewer boards seats, offer more hands-on help, and always align our incentives with yours.

📈

Conviction Investing

We make concentrated bets on companies we deeply believe in. Our average check size is $15M, and we reserve significant capital for follow-on rounds.

🧰

Long-Term Partnerships

We don't just write checks. We build decades-long relationships with our founders, supporting them through every stage of company building.

🌎

Global Perspective

With teams in San Francisco, New York, London, Berlin, Singapore, Tokyo, Tel Aviv, and São Paulo, we see global opportunities before they emerge.
